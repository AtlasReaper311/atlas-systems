Ramone — Local AI System

Architect & Systems Engineer

A fully self-hosted private AI infrastructure — zero cloud dependency, zero data egress. Five LLMs served via Ollama on local hardware, wrapped in Docker, accessed through Open WebUI with ten specialised workbots backed by RAG knowledge bases. Everything lives on a dedicated NVMe drive: portable, rebuildable in under 30 minutes.

Result

Five local models and ten specialised workbots run without cloud inference or data egress, and the stack remains portable enough to rebuild in under 30 minutes.

SONIN — Generative Audio-Visual System

Designer & DSP Engineer

An autonomous digital instrument built in Max/MSP that composes its own evolving music and visuals in real time by continuously listening and reacting to the sounds it creates. A dual-engine architecture (melodic and granular) connected by an internal audio feedback loop; the system analyses its own output and routes that data back into its own generative parameters.

Result

Two generative engines remain musically stable through an internal analysis feedback loop while GPU-rendered visuals leave the audio thread unaffected.

Atlas Systems Infrastructure — Infrastructure Dashboard & Observability

Writing seriesPipeline & ObservabilityPart 1 of 3
Infrastructure Engineer

Atlas Systems' infrastructure observes itself. A single authenticated event bus accepts pushes, deploys, and alerts from ten repos across three different webhook dialects, formats them into Discord, and persists a rolling history that a live dashboard reads directly. Five Cloudflare Workers and three Cloudflare Pages sites deploy through reusable GitHub Actions workflows defined once and shared across the whole estate; nothing reaches production without passing a real gate first. The Lab page turns that history into something visible: a 90-day commit heatmap, a classified failure log, a live status grid per repo, and a self-documenting map of every public endpoint in the system. All of it runs on free-tier infrastructure.

Result

10 repos on consistent, gated CI/CD pipelines, defined once and inherited everywhere
