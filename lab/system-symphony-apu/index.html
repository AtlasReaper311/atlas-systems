What this preview proves

State identity

Healthy, Warning, Critical and Unknown now change composition, rhythm, envelopes, role allocation and spatial treatment.

Quantised transitions

Critical chokes the old pad and counterline at the bar boundary; Unknown dissolves over one full 2.4-second bar.

Measured output

A sink-only AudioWorklet applies K-weighting, overlapping gates and peak estimation without entering the audible path.

Honest evidence

Integrated loudness is labelled by sample-rate method, while true peak remains an explicit four-times cubic estimate.
