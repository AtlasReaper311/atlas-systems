Isolate C, then write. Quorum still commits through A + B, while C remains visibly stale until you heal the link.

This is intentionally smaller than Raft or Paxos. The leader is fixed and link timings are deterministic so the interaction isolates quorum, propagation, partitions, and convergence without pretending to simulate leader election.

Choose a commit policy and network profile, then press Write Next. Quorum waits for two replicas to hold the proposal. Eventual accepts at the leader immediately and lets the followers converge later. Reset returns all replicas to version zero.
