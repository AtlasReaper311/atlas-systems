System law

Three coupled equations

The Lorenz attractor is deterministic. It contains no random-number source, yet tiny differences in state eventually produce different trajectories.

dx/dt = σ(y - x)
dy/dt = x(ρ - z) - y
dz/dt = xy - βz

σ = 10, ρ = 28, β = 8/3
