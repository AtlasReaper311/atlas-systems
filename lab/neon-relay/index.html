Challenge 01 Ignition. Phase cold. R1 OPEN, R2 OPEN, R3 OPEN, R4 OPEN. Outputs: beacon 0, archive 0, actuator 0. Circuit cold.

READY TO TEST

Circuit cold. Configure the relay route, then energise when you are ready to test it.

HOW TO PLAY

Pick a challenge, rotate the available relays, then ENERGISE. The board evaluates the route only from its current deterministic configuration.

PROTECTION

Shared traces accept one feed. Unsafe convergence, the ground dump, or a protected output opens the fuse. Fix the route before resetting protection.

CONTROLS

Click a relay to cycle OPEN → A → B. Shift + click reverses. Keys 1 to 4 select challenges. R resets exactly.
