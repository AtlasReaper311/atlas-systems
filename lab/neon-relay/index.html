CIRCUIT COLD

RELAY LEGEND

OPEN isolates a feed. A takes the direct branch. B diverts downstream. Click a relay to cycle; Shift + click cycles backwards.

PROTECTION

Each shared trace accepts one feed. Two feeds converging on R3 or R4 trip overcurrent protection. R4 / B is a deliberate short-to-ground path.

RECOVERY
