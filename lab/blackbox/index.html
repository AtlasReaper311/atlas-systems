Honesty rule

Unknown stays unknown.

Telemetry is sampled once a minute. Values between samples are visibly interpolated; event timestamps are not. A quiet graph cannot prove that an unrelated service was healthy.

A published postmortem may conclude that root cause is unresolved. The interface presents the reviewed evidence boundary instead of inventing certainty to fill the panel.
