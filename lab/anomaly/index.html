Candidate evidence

Each sample produces several imperfect clues. Median absolute deviation keeps the baseline useful when the history contains occasional spikes; slope and volatility detect motion rather than only magnitude.

Constrained alignment

The active window is compared with earlier normal windows using a Sakoe-Chiba-bounded DTW path. Derivatives carry more weight than absolute level, which makes trajectory geometry explicit without erasing amplitude evidence.

Continuity decoder

State transitions penalise implausible jumps. A single sample can raise the score, but warning and critical states require temporal support. Recovery also has to earn its way back to normal.
