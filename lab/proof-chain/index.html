Missing evidence stays missing.

A service appears here only when the public Atlas classifications and topology already identify it as a public runtime service. Unknown or non-public identifiers are not turned into discoverable records.

Cloudflare topology evidence is shown only after the sanitized atlas-resource-audit report is separately published. Until then the interface reports that evidence as unavailable rather than deriving live state from merged source.
