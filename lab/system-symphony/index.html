Interpretation boundary

What the score means

The instrument represents current public evidence. It does not infer private state, causal explanations, or health for unmeasured components.

Measured

Current service status, latency, uptime, error rate, incidents, and deployment evidence influence harmony, density, timbre, and motifs when those measurements exist.

Unmeasured

Topology-only components remain explicitly unmeasured. Neutral musical defaults preserve continuity without converting absence into a healthy claim.

Stale or unknown

Failed telemetry reads retain the last-known values for inspection while the score state becomes Unknown. The source badge and status text remain visible.

Keyboard operation

No pointer required

The inline adapter removes the modal focus trap. Native document order covers every button, range input, filter, service row, and simulation control.

  • Tab and Shift + Tab move through all controls.
  • Enter or Space activates buttons and service rows.
  • Arrow keys operate volume and performance ranges.
  • Escape closes Mapping help without dismissing the page instrument.
  • Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative animation and slow analyser updates.
  • The Service score table is the non-visual alternative to the topology graphic.
