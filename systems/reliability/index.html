Evaluate the claim

Can the estate support its reliability claims?

Reliability is presented as measured objectives and evidence quality, not a decorative green light. This route joins policy coverage, error budgets, burn states, delivery outcomes, and bounded failure experiments while retaining explicit stale, insufficient, unavailable, malformed, and unmeasured states.
